BAA OS — 5-Minute Demo

A guided walkthrough of what's actually built in BAA OS today, in the order you'd naturally explore it.

This is a written/guided walkthrough describing real, existing screens and features — not a recorded video. Every section below corresponds to something you can click through yourself on the live site right now.
0:00–0:20

Welcome to BAA OS

BAA OS is a student learning operating system: one home base for AI-guided learning, assessments, homework help, rewards, planning, and parent/teacher visibility — themed to feel personal, not generic.

0:20–0:50

Home — Student Command Center

Landing in Student OS, you see "Today's Mission" — a single AI-picked next step, your streak, level and XP, and quick access to every world below. This is the daily starting point.

0:50–1:20

Knowledge Universe

Every subject is its own explorable world. Topics are organized so a student can see, at a glance, where they stand and what's next — driven by real learning evidence, not a fixed syllabus checklist.

1:20–1:50

AI Tutor

A conversational tutor that explains concepts in the student's chosen style ("Explain like a story," "Normal Tutor," etc.), supports multiple languages, and can be asked for quick practice questions on demand.

1:50–2:20

Assessments

Quizzes and tests are auto-graded where possible and AI-evaluated for open-ended answers, with a teacher able to review and override any AI grade before it's final — nothing is silently AI-decided.

2:20–2:45

Challenge Arena

A livelier, competitive space for quick head-to-head practice — built for the moments a student wants energy and pace rather than a quiet study session.

2:45–3:10

XP, Rewards, Streak & Leaderboard

Every completed attempt, mastered concept, and finished homework earns XP and badges. Streaks track daily consistency, and a leaderboard shows how you compare with classmates.

3:10–3:35

Planner & Progress

The planner turns learning evidence into a real daily task list — practice, revision, or reassessment — instead of a generic to-do list, and tracks progress against upcoming assessments.

3:35–4:00

Virtual Labs, Coding Lab & Career

Interactive Virtual Labs let students run safe, deterministic simulations (projectile motion, Ohm's Law, quadratic exploration). Coding Lab and the Career world give students a space to explore beyond the syllabus.

4:00–4:30

Parent OS & Teacher OS

Parents get a real window into their child's learning — not just marks. Teachers get class-wide analytics, the ability to review AI-graded work, leave notes, and see evidence-based diagnostics per student.

4:30–4:50

Six Themes, Three Modes

Aurora, Galaxy, Academic, NeoGlass, Calm, and Duology — each with its own typography, color identity, and shape language — each switchable between Light, Dark, and System.

4:50–5:00

That's BAA OS

One connected system — learning, assessment, rewards, planning, and family/teacher visibility — built around real evidence of what a student actually knows.

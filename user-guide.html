BAA OS — User Guide

How to use every major part of BAA OS.

Getting started

Account / Login

Use "Sign Up" to create an account (name, email, password) or "Log In" if you already have one. Your account is stored on BAA's server, not just in your browser. Forgot your password? Use the "Forgot password?" link on the Log In tab.

Student sections

Home

Your daily starting point — today's AI-picked mission, streak, level, and XP, with quick access to every other section.

Learn / Knowledge Universe

Every subject as its own world, showing where you stand on each topic based on real evidence from your attempts.

AI Tutor

Ask questions in plain language. Choose your explanation style ("Normal Tutor," "A Story," etc.) and language from the dropdowns above the chat box.

Assessments

Take quizzes and tests. Auto-graded questions are marked instantly; open-ended answers are AI-evaluated and can be reviewed by your teacher.

Challenge Arena

Fast-paced competitive practice for when you want energy over a quiet study session.

XP & Rewards

Earn XP and badges for attempts, mastered concepts, and completed homework. Track your streak and see how you rank on the leaderboard.

Planner

A daily task list generated from your actual learning evidence — practice, revision, or reassessment — not a generic checklist.

Progress

See your standing per subject/chapter, including anything currently blocking you from moving to the next chapter (a "mastery gate") until it's cleared.

Virtual Labs

Run safe, deterministic simulations — projectile motion, Ohm's Law, and quadratic exploration — to see concepts in action.

Coding Lab & Career

Explore programming and career-path content beyond the core syllabus.

Parent & Teacher

Parent OS

See your child's real learning progress and evidence — not just scores — and stay informed on what they're working through.

Teacher OS

Class-wide analytics, the ability to review and override AI-graded work, and a space to leave notes on individual students.

Themes

Open the theme button (top corner of any page) to switch between six visual identities, each in Light, Dark, or System mode.

⭐ Aurora

The flagship BAA identity — premium, atmospheric, cyan/violet/gold accents.

🌌 Galaxy

A more playful, exploration-driven feel for student adventure.

📚 Academic

Clean, structured, and professional — built for parents, teachers, and schools.

💎 NeoGlass

Premium glassmorphism — translucent, layered surfaces.

🌿 Calm

Soft, low-intensity, focus-friendly surfaces for wellbeing.

🧸 Duology

Bright, rounded, and friendly — built for a younger audience, with gentle motion that respects reduced-motion settings.

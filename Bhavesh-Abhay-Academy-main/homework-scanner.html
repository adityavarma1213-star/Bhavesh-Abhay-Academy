What this checkpoint actually does.
  • ✅ Typed or pasted homework text — saved to your submission history, right here in this browser.
  • ✅ Optional photo attachment — select, preview, remove/replace, and it's compressed and prepared right here in your browser. Not evaluated yet, and not stored beyond this submission (see privacy note below the form).
  • ✅ Automatic AI evaluation — text and attached image content are evaluated through the M8-B1/B2 server vision path. Unreadable or low-confidence images are sent to human review.
  • ✅ PDF support — M8-C extracts selectable text from PDFs in your browser and sends only the extracted text through the existing evaluation path. The original PDF bytes are not persisted.
  • ⚠️ Scanned/image-only PDFs — if no selectable text can be extracted, the PDF is rejected honestly rather than pretending OCR/evaluation occurred.
